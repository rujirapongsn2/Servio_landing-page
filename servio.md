# Servio by Softnix - AI Customer Support Agent

## Product Overview

Servio is an intelligent AI customer support system developed by Softnix Technology. It represents a multi-agent architecture approach to customer service, combining voice, chat, and text capabilities in a seamless omnichannel experience.

**Tagline:** "ยกระดับบริการลูกค้าด้วย AI ที่ "ฟัง-พูด-พิมพ์" ได้ครบวงจร"
**Translation:** Elevate customer service with AI that listens, speaks, and types comprehensively.

## Multi-Agent Architecture

### Architecture Overview

Servio uses a **Hub-and-Spoke structure** where:
- Each AI agent has specialized responsibilities (like real departments)
- Agents are coordinated by a central coordinator
- They work together for accuracy and efficiency in service delivery

### Core Agent Types

#### 1. **Coordinator Agent** - "ด่านหน้าอัจฉริยะ" (Intelligent Front Gate)
- First point of contact for customers
- Routes customer inquiries to the appropriate specialized agent
- Analyzes customer problems and channels them to the correct department
- Role: Smart receptionist and problem classification

#### 2. **Sales Agent** - "ที่ปรึกษาการขาย" (Sales Consultant)
- Answers product questions accurately
- Provides product information based on knowledge base
- Stimulates purchasing decisions with correct data
- Leads qualification and upselling capability

#### 3. **Support Agent** - "ฝ่ายบริการลูกค้า" (Customer Service Department)
- Handles usage issues and troubleshooting
- Verifies service status and order status
- Manages complex cases quickly and efficiently
- Provides 24/7 support

#### 4. **Delivery Agent** - "ฝ่ายให้ข้อมูลขนส่ง" (Logistics Information Department)
- Tracks shipment status
- Provides delivery information
- Handles logistics-related inquiries

## Use Cases by Industry

### 1. **Customer Support Services**

#### Retail & E-Commerce
- **Scenario:** Customers inquire about package status or file product claims
- **Servio Solution:** Support Agent connects to shipping API for instant updates; Claim Agent verifies conditions from Knowledge Base and auto-approves initial claims

#### Banking & Insurance
- **Scenario:** Customers need emergency card blocking or policy coverage questions
- **Servio Solution:** Voice Command for fast card blocking; RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) for complex policy information; 24/7 availability

### 2. **Sales & Reservations**

#### Hospitality & Healthcare
- **Scenario:** Customers call to check availability, schedule appointments, or book services
- **Servio Solution:** Reception Agent checks real-time availability via API and creates appointments instantly; SMS confirmation sent automatically

#### Real Estate & Automotive
- **Scenario:** Customers interested in new properties or vehicle models
- **Servio Solution:** Sales Agent qualifies leads, provides specifications and promotions, schedules showroom visits when interest is high

### 3. **Internal Operations**

#### Corporate HR Support
- **Scenario:** Employees ask about vacation rights, medical expense claims, or work policies
- **Servio Solution:** HR Agent searches Employee Handbook from File Store; instant answers reduce HR department workload

#### IT Helpdesk
- **Scenario:** Employees report computer issues, forgotten passwords, or WiFi problems
- **Servio Solution:** Technical Agent provides step-by-step troubleshooting; escalates to IT staff if needed via automatic Ticket creation

### 4. **Public Services**

#### Government Agencies
- **Scenario:** Citizens inquire about required documents or tax procedures
- **Servio Solution:** Voice Agent helps elderly/non-typing citizens; provides accurate government information 24/7 based on official regulations

#### Utility Services
- **Scenario:** Report power failures, burst pipes, or check service charges
- **Servio Solution:** Intake complaint, identify location, and automatically coordinate maintenance teams; track and notify citizens of progress

### 5. **SME & Social Commerce**

#### Live Collaboration Mode
- **Scenario:** Customer asks about shirt sizes or bulk pricing while shop owner is busy
- **Servio Solution:** Owner sees AI conversation in Monitor; can instantly Takeover to respond when customer hesitates or asks complex questions

#### Auto-Pilot Mode
- **Scenario:** Customer orders at 2 AM or when owner wants to rest
- **Servio Solution:** Set status to "Off-duty"; AI handles 100% of customer interaction (questions, orders, payment summaries); store operates 24/7

**Tagline for SME:** "ไม่ต้องจ้างแอดมินกะดึก ให้ Servio เฝ้าร้านแทนคุณ รับออเดอร์ได้แม้ตอนคุณหลับ"
**Translation:** "No need to hire a night shift admin - let Servio guard your store and receive orders even while you sleep"
